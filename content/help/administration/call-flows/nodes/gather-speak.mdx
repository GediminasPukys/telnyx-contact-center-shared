---
title: Gather Using Speak
description: Speak a prompt and collect keypad input in one operation.
---

**Gather Using Speak** combines text-to-speech prompting with DTMF collection. Configure prompt voice/text plus digit, terminator, timeout, and retry behavior.

Keep the prompt aligned with accepted input and provide replay/invalid-input paths. Test callers who enter digits before playback finishes and those who provide no input.

Soniox voices are multilingual: the voice sets the accent and **Speech Language** (shown when a Soniox voice is selected) sets the pronunciation, for example Lithuanian (`lt`). Without it Soniox speaks English. **Test Voice** previews the selected language.
