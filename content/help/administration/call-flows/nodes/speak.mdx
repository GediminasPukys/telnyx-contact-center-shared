---
title: Speak Text
description: Synthesize text to speech on the call.
---

**Speak Text** renders configured text or a template using the selected voice/language settings.

Validate every variable used in the text, provide a fallback for missing personalization, and keep prompts short. Connect playback completion and error/interruption outputs when subsequent behavior differs.

Soniox voices are multilingual: the voice sets the accent and **Speech Language** (shown when a Soniox voice is selected) sets the pronunciation, for example Lithuanian (`lt`). Without it Soniox speaks English. **Test Voice** previews the selected language.
